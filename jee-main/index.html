Frequently asked questions

Is SONEXA free for JEE Main practice?

Yes. All JEE Main papers on SONEXA are free to practise, with no login or sign-up. Open a paper and start the test.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login. Your scores, mistakes and streak are saved only on your own phone or browser.

Are these official JEE Main papers?

Yes. JEE Main papers are the official papers, with answers checked against the official answer keys.

Do I get the answer key and my score?

Yes. When you submit, you see your score with the official marking scheme, every right and wrong answer, the correct answer and a full review of the paper.

Can I practise chapter-wise?

Yes. SONEXA groups the JEE Main previous-year questions by chapter, so you can practise all questions of one chapter across years in one test.

Does it work offline or as an app?

Yes. Tap “Save offline” on any paper to use it without internet, and Android users can install the SONEXA app from the App page.
